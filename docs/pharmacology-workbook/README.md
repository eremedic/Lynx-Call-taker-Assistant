# Medical Math Workbook for Paramedic Students

A student workbook covering drug math, IV fluids and infusion pumps for paramedic programs.

- `Medical-Math-Workbook.pdf` — the book: full-bleed front and back covers, title and copyright pages, contents with page numbers, 12 chapters (about 230 practice problems), US Letter.
- `Medical-Math-Workbook.html` — the same workbook as a web page (open in any browser).

## Contents

1. Medication safety foundations (rights, safe notation, rounding)
2. The metric system and conversions
3. IV fluids in the paramedic scope (compartments, tonicity, NS, LR, D5W, D10, ½NS, balanced solutions, catheters, drip sets)
4. Dose and volume: how much to draw up (D/H × Q, three solving methods)
5. Weight-based calculations
6. Drip rates I: volume over time
7. Drip rates II: concentration infusions (mcg/min, mcg/kg/min, dose from rate)
8. Percent and ratio concentrations, dilutions (C₁V₁ = C₂V₂)
9. IV infusion pumps (parts, terms, programming, secondary infusions, alarms, transports)
10. Putting it all together: 100 mixed problems, weighted toward dose/volume, drip rates and concentrations
11. Formula reference card
12. Answer key with worked solutions (every practice problem)

Doses and concentrations are teaching examples. Always follow local protocols and medical direction.
